# Naming doctrine:
#   Destroyers       : weather and violent natural phenomena
#   Submarines       : predators and birds of prey
#   Light cruisers   : cities
#   Heavy cruisers   : regions and historic lands
#   Battlecruisers   : battles and military victories
#   Battleships      : rulers, statesmen or ideological symbols
#   Aircraft carriers: national and ideological symbols
#
# The historical 1936 monarchy is represented by neutrality, while the
# alternative royal route may use the custom monarchist ideology.
